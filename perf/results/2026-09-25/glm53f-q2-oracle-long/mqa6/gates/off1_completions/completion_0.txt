  and quantize. Prints one line per phase with median and spread. Useful for
  locating which phase absorbs a regression.
- `VLLM_QC_PHASE_PROF=1 VLLM_QC_PHASE_PROF_DETAIL=1` — adds per-iteration
  detail lines, including per-phase breakdown and per-iteration medians. Use
  for short runs; detail output is verbose.

Both profilers are diagnostics only. They are not retained performance wins
and must not be recorded as such.
