# Dependencies for Apple Silicon (Metal / PyTorch-MPS).
#
# Deliberately thin next to cuda.txt and rocm.txt. Most of what those pull in
# is unavailable or meaningless here:
#   - no triton: it does not target Metal. The batch-prep helpers vLLM ships as
#     Triton kernels are replaced with torch equivalents in
#     vllm/platforms/metal_compat.py.
#   - no nccl/amdsmi/pynvml: one device, no collectives, and the Apple probe is
#     two sysctl reads.
#   - no flash-attn/deep_gemm/flashinfer: CUDA-only, and their roles here
#     belong to the vendored QuixiCore-Metal kernels in csrc/quixicore/metal/.

# torch with MPS. 2.13 is what the Metal kernels are developed and tested
# against; bf16 support on MPS is the reason for the floor.
torch == 2.13.0

# GGUF metadata parsing for the loader and the config parsers.
gguf >= 0.10.0
